# Code of Conduct

Hexicon adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome beginners, and keep criticism focused on content — not people. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing and sharing others' private data are not tolerated.

**Community-specific rules**

- No requests for help attacking systems you are not authorized to test.
- No sharing of malware, leaked credentials, stolen data or pirated material.
- Contributions in any of our languages are equally welcome.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainers privately via GitHub (the "Report content" option, or a private security advisory for sensitive matters). Reports are handled confidentially. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant guidelines.
